\subsection*{Dress code}
We politely suggest that you wear a somewhat formal outfit, with no bright colors
that would mess up the photographs at the event (no clown costumes and
{\it RHCP} T-shirts this time, unfortunately). For ladies: pastel colors, if you will.

\subsection*{Alcohol}
A basic set of alcoholic drinks will be provided at the banquet,
including champagne, wine, and vodka.
You are free (and actually encouraged!) to sneak your own {\it Жигулёвское} into the hall.

\subsection*{Feedback}
We would like to hear about your preferences, that include but are not limited to:
\begin{itemize}
  \item what music you'd like to have in the playlist at the banquet;
  \item if you have any ideas on fun games and challenges for the evening;
  \item whatever details you would like to add.
\end{itemize}
